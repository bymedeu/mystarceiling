# Development history — 2026-09-13

## Catalogue provenance and identity metadata

**Choices discussed:** reserve Gaia identifier bits for provenance; or preserve
the native `u64` identifier and add separate `u8` metadata. A possible split of
three catalogue-source bits and five classification bits was raised.

**Current status:** separate `u8` metadata is recommended but not yet approved or
implemented. The meaning and taxonomy of “classification,” the bit allocation,
and the in-memory layout remain open.

**Owner's reason:** retaining more source options may be useful if MSC adds
catalogues beyond Gaia and Hipparcos. The owner also wants identifiers excluded
from intensive stellar calculations where practical.

**Trade-off recorded:** one logical byte may consume more space in an
array-of-objects layout because of alignment. A later structure-of-arrays or cold
metadata table could store provenance separately from the calculation loop, but
that remains a measured performance decision.

## Proper-motion propagation

**Choices discussed:** divide the published right-ascension motion by
`cos(declination)` and special-case the poles; apply modulo to the coordinate;
or propagate a three-dimensional tangent vector and wrap right ascension only
afterward.

**Owner decision:** use tangent-vector propagation.

**Owner's reason:** it is the established pole-safe solution and is acceptable
even though the full mathematics is not yet completely familiar to the owner.

**Implementation:** moved source-independent proper-motion and reference-epoch
types from the catalogue layer into the core, grouped the two named components,
and added a pure propagation operation. The operation assumes zero radial motion,
does not use parallax, does not modify `icrs_to_enu`, and is not yet called by the
M1 application. Detailed rationale is in
[the stellar-motion decision](../decisions/stellar-motion.md).

**Validation:** the primary AI inspected and corrected a bounded patch produced
by the secondary `dcodex` account. Corrections made the component names explicit,
separated error reasons, preserved zero-motion inputs, and removed an unsupported
claim of direct ERFA validation. The project then built successfully and all 106
tests passed.

## Propagation logging

**Choices discussed:** per-star debug or trace logging inside the calculation;
or aggregate reporting and sampled failures at the future batch/application
boundary.

**Decision:** keep the core mathematical operation silent. Future bulk code will
own aggregate failure counts and timing; detailed record logs will be sampled or
explicitly enabled.

**Reason:** formatting, synchronization, and output can dominate a loop operating
on millions of stars. Even disabled runtime log calls add a level check to the
hot path. Pure return values also make the operation easier to test and benchmark.
